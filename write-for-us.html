Write for Us

Epic Roofing & Construction LLC • Houston, Texas

Are you a roofing professional, contractor, or home-improvement writer with genuine expertise to share? If you have been searching for a roofing “write for us” opportunity, you have found it. Epic Roofing TX welcomes expert contributors who can help Houston-area homeowners make smarter decisions about their roofs.

Our blog covers the questions Greater Houston homeowners actually ask — storm and hail damage, roof repair versus replacement, metal roofing, insurance claims, and honest maintenance advice. If you can add real, experience-based value on topics like these, we offer three ways to work with us.

Three Ways to Contribute

Paid Placement

1. Sponsored Post

A full article published on the Epic Roofing TX blog that promotes your business, product, or service to Houston homeowners.

  • Permanent post — it stays live on our blog
  • Up to 2 dofollow links to your website
  • Commercial intent allowed — promote your offer openly
  • Priority editorial queue with faster review and publishing

Pricing: email us for current rates.

Paid Placement

2. Link Insertion

Already have a page that needs a relevant roofing link? We can place your link inside an existing, published Epic Roofing TX article where it fits the topic naturally.

  • Placement inside a live, established post
  • Natural, in-content anchor text
  • Permanent placement
  • Subject to topic and site relevance checks

Pricing: email us for current rates.

Free

3. Guest Post

Our free guest post option is reserved for genuinely strong, fully on-topic articles that teach homeowners something useful — not thinly disguised advertisements.

  • No placement fee
  • Author credit on the published article
  • Up to 2 relevant links, subject to editorial review
  • Strict quality bar — most promotional pitches are redirected to Sponsored Posts

Pricing: free for accepted articles.

Topics We Accept

Roofing

  • Roof repair and roof replacement guides
  • Shingle roofing vs. metal roofing comparisons
  • Storm, hail, and wind damage — assessment, repair, and prevention
  • Roof inspection and roof maintenance advice
  • Roof insurance claim guides for homeowners
  • Chimney flashing and roof flashing
  • Skylight leaks and skylight care
  • Gutter installation, repair, and maintenance
  • Attic ventilation and roof ventilation

Houston & Texas Local Angles

  • Hurricane-season roof preparation for Gulf Coast homes
  • Attic heat, insulation, and energy-saving upgrades for Texas summers
  • Texas building codes, permits, and HOA rules that affect roofing projects

Adjacent Home-Exterior Topics (Limited)

We occasionally accept articles on siding, windows, and exterior painting — only when the article connects clearly to roofing or to the home’s exterior as a whole. Purely interior topics are out of scope.

Topics We Do Not Accept

To protect our readers and our contributors, we cannot publish content about casinos or gambling, cryptocurrency, loans or payday finance, pharmaceuticals, essay-writing services, CBD products, adult content, or any topic unrelated to homes, roofing, or home improvement. Please don’t send pitches on these subjects — they won’t receive a reply.

Submission Guidelines

  • Length: 1,000+ words, covering the topic in genuine depth.
  • Originality: 100% original content that has never been published anywhere else. No copied, spun, or recycled articles — every submission is checked.
  • Structure: clear headings using H2 and H3 subheadings, with short, readable paragraphs.
  • Images: at least 2 relevant images that you own or have the rights to use.
  • Links: a maximum of 2 links per post, and they must be relevant to the article.
  • Language: US English, written for homeowners — plain language first, jargon explained.
  • Editorial review: every submission is reviewed before publishing, and we may edit for clarity, accuracy, and house style.
  • Response time: we reply to every on-topic inquiry within 48 hours.

How to Submit

Send your pitch or finished draft to our editorial desk at contact@epicroofingtx.com with the subject line:

Guest Post Inquiry – [Proposed Topic]

In your email, please tell us:

  • Which option you are interested in: Guest Post, Sponsored Post, or Link Insertion
  • Your proposed topic — or, for a link insertion, the existing post you have in mind
  • The website your links will point to

We look forward to reading your work. If you would rather talk it through first, call us at (281) 326-9905 (Mon–Sat 7AM–7PM) or use our Contact Page.
