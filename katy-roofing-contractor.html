What typical roof repair katy tx issues do homeowners face due to soil shifting? ✕
Katy's highly expansive clay soils expand and contract aggressively. This sub-grade shifting transitions directly into foundation and wall movement, which pulls on roof trusses. This movement often causes fascia boards, shingle lines, and crucial metal drip edges to separate, creating subtle water routing leaks that rotting timber under-decks require fixing.
