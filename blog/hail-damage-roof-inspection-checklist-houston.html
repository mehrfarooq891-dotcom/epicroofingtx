Hail Damage Roof Inspection Checklist
for Houston Homes

When severe spring thunderstorms drop quarter-sized hail across Cypress, Katy, or Spring, inspecting your property quickly is essential. Follow this 15-point ground-level and attic inspection checklist to document hail strikes without risking your personal safety.

📅 Published: August 7, 2026 ⏱️ 10 Min Read 🏷️ Category: Storm & Hail Damage

When severe thunderstorms roll through Greater Houston, hail stones ranging from dime-sized pellets to golf-ball-sized ice impact residential roofs at speeds over 70 MPH. While dramatic roof destruction is easy to spot, moderate hail impacts often leave quiet, subtle damage that leads to major water intrusion months later during heavy rain downpours.

You do not need to climb onto a steep, slippery roof with a extension ladder to evaluate your home after a severe hail storm. In fact, professional insurance adjusters always begin their evaluation on ground level by checking "soft metals" and surrounding collateral property assets.

This 15-point, 4-stage inspection checklist is designed specifically for Houston homeowners. Work through these steps in order to determine whether your property requires professional storm restoration and insurance documentation.

Stage 1: Ground-Level & Collateral Property Checklist

Soft metals like aluminum downspouts, mailboxes, and outdoor AC condenser units serve as ideal "hail indicators." Because thin aluminum shows clear dents upon impact, documenting these items provides proof of hail size and directionality for your insurance claim.

Stage 1 Inspection Points:

  1. Item 1 — Air Conditioner Condensing Fins: Inspect the thin metal cooling fins on your outdoor HVAC unit. Heavy hail impacts bend fins, blocking airflow and creating distinct dent patterns.
  2. Item 2 — Metal Downspouts & Splash Blocks: Check aluminum downspout pipes at eye level. Dents on the weather-facing side confirm hail stone size.
  3. Item 3 — Mailbox & Outdoor Light Fixtures: Examine metal mailbox tops, porch light shrouds, and decorative yard fixtures for impact dings or cracked glass.
  4. Item 4 — Vinyl Siding & Window Screens: Look for chips, hairline cracks, or puncture holes in window screens and vinyl siding panels facing the windward side of the storm.
  5. Item 5 — Wooden Decks & Fence Panels: Check outdoor wooden fences for fresh, unweathered circular impact marks where hail stripped away paint, stain, or algae.

If you notice clear impact dings across three or more items on this ground-level list, there is a 90%+ probability that your roof shingles suffered corresponding impact bruises. When collateral damage is present, requesting professional storm damage assessment services is the logical next step.

Stage 2: Roofline & Gutter System Checklist

Your gutter system collects the protective mineral granules that hail stones knock loose from asphalt shingles. Examining gutters from ground level gives crucial clues about shingle deterioration.

Stage 2 Inspection Points:

  1. Item 6 — Gutter Trough Dents: Look up at the bottom and face of your seamless aluminum gutters. Circular indentations along the gutter belly indicate heavy direct hits.
  2. Item 7 — Downspout Granule Accumulation: Check where downspouts discharge onto splash blocks or driveway concrete. Heavy piles of black or grey shingle granules indicate severe asphalt exposure.
  3. Item 8 — Drip Edge Metal Deformation: Inspect the thin metal flashing running along the roof eave edge. Denting here shows direct hail impact at shingle overhang points.
  4. Item 9 — Gutter Guard Screen Damage: If you have aluminum mesh or plastic gutter covers, check for dislodged screens, crushed mesh, or cracked covers.

Stage 3: Attic & Interior Inspection Checklist

Hail damage creates microscopic fractures in asphalt shingle fiberglass backing. Water does not always leak through drywall immediately, but interior attic checks can catch active moisture early.

Stage 3 Inspection Points:

  1. Item 10 — Plywood Sheathing Dampness: Use a flashlight to inspect the underside of roof decking in your attic after rain. Look for wet dark spots around nails or seam joints.
  2. Item 11 — Insulation Discoloration: Check yellow or pink blown-in fiberglass insulation under valley seams for dampness or matted clumps.
  3. Item 12 — Ceiling & Drywall Water Rings: Inspect upper floor ceilings and interior closets for faint yellow or brown water rings around light fixtures and vent fans. If an active leak is leaking through sheetrock, securing professional roof leak repair prevents wood rot and mold.

Stage 4: Rooftop Shingle & Penetration Checklist (Professional Scope)

Note: For personal safety, homeowners should leave rooftop walking to licensed, safety-harnessed roofers. This section outlines what a certified inspector evaluates when walking your roof deck.

Roof Asset What Inspector Looks For Consequence of Unrepaired Damage
Item 13 — Asphalt Shingle Mat Dark, bruised indentation spots where protective mineral granules were dislodged, exposing raw asphalt matting. Solar UV rays bake exposed asphalt, causing shingle cracking and leaks within 6–12 months.
Item 14 — Roof Vents & Turtle Caps Dented aluminum attic ridge vents, cracked plastic turtle vents, and crushed pipe boot collars. Dented vents leak rainwater directly into attic insulation and ceiling drywall.
Item 15 — Ridge Cap Shingles Fractured shingle caps along roof peaks. Hail strikes along ridges often crack shingle backing completely in half. Ridge leaks penetrate the highest point of the home, running down rafters to multiple rooms.

What to Do After Completing Your Inspection Checklist

If your completed checklist confirms collateral dents, heavy granule buildup in downspouts, or indoor moisture signs, take action promptly:

  • Step A — Take Timestamped Photos: Photograph every dented downspout, damaged window screen, and granule pile.
  • Step B — Review Policy Timelines: Most Texas homeowners insurance policies require filing hail claims within 12 months of the storm date. Understanding the Texas roof insurance claim timeline ensures you do not miss coverage deadlines.
  • Step C — Secure Emergency Tarping if Needed: If hail punctured skylights, plastic vents, or missing shingle sections, applying emergency tarp stabilization prevents catastrophic rain damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can small hail under 1 inch in diameter actually damage architectural shingles?

Yes. While smaller hail may not puncture shingles, hail combined with high wind speeds (50+ MPH) strips away granule coatings and fractures shingle seals, significantly shortening roof lifespan.

Why does my insurance adjuster need to see collateral damage like dented AC units?

Adjusters look for collateral evidence to verify that hail struck the property on the specific storm date claimed. Clear dents on soft metals validate your shingle damage claim.

Is loss of shingle granules considered covered hail damage in Texas?

If hail impacts dislodge granules and expose the underlying black asphalt mat, insurance carriers classify it as functional damage because the shingle’s waterproofing capability is permanently degraded.

How long after a Houston hail storm should I get a professional inspection?

It is best to request an inspection within 14 to 30 days following a storm. Early documentation catches minor leaks before mold develops and establishes clear proof for insurance adjusters.
