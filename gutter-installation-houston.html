Why Gutters Matter Specifically in Houston

Houston receives over 50 inches of annual rainfall, often delivered in intense tropical downpours and severe thunderstorms. Without properly sized and pitched gutters, this water volume creates costly structural damage.

💡 Houston Drainage Fact: A 2,000 sq ft roof sheds roughly 1,250 gallons of water for every single inch of rain. In a 3-inch Houston downpour, your gutters must manage and safely redirect over 3,700 gallons away from your slab and roof edge.

💧

Heavy Rain Volume Management

Standard 5-inch sectioned gutters easily overflow during Gulf Coast cloudbursts. We install oversized 6-inch seamless systems with heavy-flow downspouts to handle high water volume without backing up.

🏡

Foundation & Slab Protection

Houston's expansive clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry. Uncontrolled roof runoff dumping right at the foundation line causes soil erosion, slab shifting, interior drywall cracks, and costly leveling repairs.

🛡️

Roof Edge & Landscape Defense

Seamless gutters prevent trenching around flower beds, siding discoloration from splash-back, and water creeping behind the drip edge into your decking. Learn more about our comprehensive Houston roofing solutions.

How Damaged Gutters Threaten Your Entire Roof System

Your gutters are not an isolated accessory—they are the first line of defense for your roof perimeter.

The Hidden Danger of Fascia Board & Eaves Decay

When gutters clog with debris or pull loose from old spike hangers, standing water pools along the roof eaves. In Houston's high-humidity climate, this trapped moisture constantly wets the wooden fascia board and soffit framing.

Over time, this moisture wicks upward past the metal drip edge into the starter row of plywood roof decking. What began as a minor gutter sag quickly develops into extensive rot, mold growth in the attic, and expensive perimeter repairs.
