Understanding Houston Roofing Calculations

How contractors in Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery counties utilize pitch, slope, and roofing squares to estimate storm restoration.

What Is a Roofing Square?

In the roofing trade, a "square" is a unit of measurement equal to exactly 100 square feet (10' × 10'). A typical 2,000 sq ft home with eaves, pitch adjustment, and garage overhangs usually requires between 24 and 30 roofing squares of material.

Why Pitch Matters for Hurricanes

Roofs between 4:12 and 7:12 offer the best aerodynamic balance for Gulf Coast winds. Low-pitch roofs (<4:12) face extreme uplift pressures on eaves, while ultra-steep roofs (>9:12) take higher lateral wind loading from hurricane-force gusts.

Waste Factors on Complex Hips

Simple two-sided gable roofs need only 7% to 10% waste material. Complex hip roofs with dormers, turrets, and multiple valleys require 12% to 18% extra material to ensure continuous starter courses and ridge cap coverage.
