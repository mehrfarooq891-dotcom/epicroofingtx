💡 The Short Answer

A full roof replacement in Houston averages $9,752 according to Angi, and most homeowners pay somewhere between $6,712 and $13,123. Per square foot, expect roughly $4 to $6 for asphalt shingles in Houston, $5 to $16 for metal, $7 to $25 for tile, and $4 to $10 for TPO on low-slope roofs. The two things that move your number most are the size of your roof and the material you pick — everything else on this page explains the rest.

Why the ranges are wide — and why sources disagree. What Drives Roof Replacement Cost in Houston?

Two homes on the same street can get quotes thousands of dollars apart. These are the line items that explain why.

🌀

Storm & Hurricane Code Requirements

Harris County and Texas Department of Insurance coastal standards call for shingles rated for 110 to 130 mph winds (ASTM D7158 Class H), installed with six-nail fastening patterns and approved starter strips. Wind-rated materials cost more than builder-grade — Angi notes impact- and wind-resistant shingles sit near $6 per sq ft — and homes inside the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association (TWIA) catastrophe area, which includes parts of Harris County east of Highway 146, also need a WPI-8 windstorm inspection certificate to keep TWIA coverage. Skipping code items makes a quote cheaper and a roof weaker; it can also void coverage.

🗑️

Tear-Off & Disposal

Most quotes include removing the old roof, but not all. Angi puts removal and haul-away at $1,000 to $3,000 when it is billed separately; HomeGuide puts shingle and metal tear-off at roughly $1 to $3 per sq ft, with heavy tile and slate costing more. A second existing layer costs more to strip than one. If a quote looks unusually low, check whether tear-off is actually in it.

🪵

Decking Condition Underneath

Houston gets nearly 52 inches of rain a year — more than 1.5 times the national average — so hidden water damage is the most common surprise on a Houston tear-off. Angi budgets an additional $500 to $2,000 to replace damaged decking found once the old roof is off. Nobody can price rotted decking honestly before the tear-off exposes it, so a fair quote names a per-sheet decking rate instead of pretending the deck is fine.

📐

Pitch, Storeys & Complexity

Steep slopes, two-storey access, and roofs cut up by valleys, dormers, chimneys and skylights all take more labour hours and more safety setup than a simple single-storey gable. Every penetration is also a flashing detail that has to be rebuilt. This is why per-square-foot figures are planning ranges, not promises.

📄

Permits

A replacement inside Houston city limits is permitted work. Per Angi, the City of Houston charges a base fee of $46.35 for the first $7,000 of work plus $5.29 for each additional $1,000 — about $62 on an average replacement. Your roofer normally pulls the permit and passes the fee through on the invoice. Homes in unincorporated Harris County and surrounding municipalities follow their own rules and fees.

👷

Labour's Share of the Quote

On a typical Houston replacement, Angi expects about $4,800 to $5,900 of the total to be labour. That is the part of the quote that buys correct fastening patterns, sealed valleys and clean flashing work — the details that decide whether the new roof survives its first hurricane season. The cheapest labour line is rarely the cheapest roof over ten years.

How Long Does a Roof Replacement Take?

The installation itself is the short part. For most standard Houston homes, the old roof is torn off and the new one installed in 1 to 2 working days. Tile and custom standing seam metal layouts typically take 3 to 5 days. Around those working days, allow time for the steps that happen off the roof:

1

Inspection, Measurement & Written Quote

The roof is walked and measured, damage is photographed, and you get an itemised written quote. If storm damage is involved, add time for the insurance adjuster's visit and scope decision.

2

Permit & Materials

The permit is pulled and materials are ordered and delivered. In peak storm season, both queues get longer — booking right after a major hail or hurricane event can add weeks, not days.

3

Tear-Off & Installation

Old layers come off, the deck is inspected and repaired where needed, and the new system goes down. Houston weather can pause a job mid-way — a competent crew will not leave a torn-off roof open ahead of rain.

4

Cleanup & Final Walkthrough

Debris is hauled away, the yard is swept for nails, and the finished roof is walked and photographed. Keep the final invoice and material documentation with your insurance records.

When Is a Repair Enough Instead?

Not every bad roof needs replacing. Paying for a full replacement on a roof with years of life left wastes as much money as patching a roof that is finished. The honest split:

👍 A Repair Is Usually Enough When…

  • ✓ The roof is under 10–12 years old and otherwise healthy
  • ✓ Damage is isolated — one pipe boot, one flashing point, a small wind-lifted section
  • ✓ The leak traces to a single spot, not several ceilings at once
  • ✓ The underlayment and decking are still sound
  • ✓ You need a few more sound years before a planned replacement

Typical Houston repairs run $300–$600 for minor work. See roof repair in Houston for repair pricing and what each repair covers.

⚠️ Replacement Is Usually the Better Buy When…

  • ✓ The roof is 15–20+ years old — in Houston's heat, shingles age faster than the national average
  • ✓ Storm damage or wear covers more than about 25–30% of the surface
  • ✓ Leaks keep coming back in different places
  • ✓ Decking is soft, sagging or rotted
  • ✓ Repair quotes are climbing toward a third of replacement cost

HomeGuide's rule of thumb says the same thing: replacement is often the better buy when the roof is over 20 years old, the repair estimate tops $3,000, or damage covers more than a quarter of the roof. Our full decision guide: roof repair vs. replacement in Houston.
