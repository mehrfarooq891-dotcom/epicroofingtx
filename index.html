Forensic Diagnostics

Hidden Damage You Cannot See From The Ground

Most storm damage never produces an immediate water drip inside your living room. The structural degradation happens quietly under the surface.

Defect 01

Hail Bruising

Hail impacts crush the shingle's asphalt matrix without punching a visible hole. The crushed area loses its protective granules over the next 6 to 18 months, leaving the fiberglass mat exposed to UV rot and leaks long after storm filing deadlines expire.

Defect 02

Wind Uplift Damage

Straight-line gusts break the factory sealant strip bonding shingle tabs together. The shingles flap in the breeze and crease along the top edge. When rain follows, water tracks backwards under the unsealed tabs directly onto the wood decking.

Defect 03

Moisture & Rot

Slow leaks around chimney step flashing, plumbing vent collars, or valley seams saturate attic insulation and plywood sheathing. Wood rot softens the roof deck and breeds mold before a ceiling drywall stain ever appears.

Direct Answers

Texas Insurance & Storm Damage Facts

Plain facts on hail detection, claim eligibility, and policy terms without sales speak.

How do I know if my roof has hail damage?

Hail damage on asphalt shingles appears as dark, bruised circular indentations where protective mineral granules were knocked away, exposing the fragile fiberglass mat beneath. On metal vents, flashing, and gutters, hail leaves distinct dings and dents. Because granule loss and micro-fractures are difficult to spot from the ground, a physical roof-level inspection within 12 months of a storm is essential to document evidence before carrier filing deadlines pass.

Will my insurance cover roof damage in Texas?

Standard Texas homeowners insurance policies cover sudden, accidental storm damage caused by hail, hurricane-force wind, and falling tree limbs. Policies do not cover normal wear and tear, cosmetic aging, or pre-existing leaks caused by neglected maintenance. If damage covers a significant portion of your roof slope, your insurer is generally required to pay for the complete replacement of that slope or the entire roof structure minus your deductible.

What's the difference between ACV and RCV on my roof policy?

An Actual Cash Value (ACV) policy only pays what your roof is worth at the time of the storm, deducting years of depreciation from your payout and leaving you to cover the gap out of pocket. A Replacement Cost Value (RCV) policy pays the full current cost to replace your roof with new materials of like kind and quality once the contractor completes the work, holding back recoverable depreciation until final invoice submission.

How do I know if I need roof repair or full replacement?

If your roof is under 12 to 15 years old and the damage is confined to a single localized area (such as a loose pipe boot or isolated flashing issue), a targeted repair is appropriate. If the roof is over 15 years old, shows widespread granule depletion, has brittle shingles that crack when lifted, or suffered hail impacts across more than 25% to 30% of its surface, a complete replacement provides long-term protection and meets Texas windstorm building codes.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most standard residential roof replacements in the Greater Houston area are completed in 1 to 2 days. Our crew removes the old shingles down to the plywood decking, replaces any rotted decking boards, installs synthetic underlayment and ice/water barrier in valleys, nails down the new shingle system, and completes magnetic sweeps of your yard to collect every stray nail.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Houston?

Yes. The City of Houston and surrounding municipal jurisdictions require a building permit for complete residential re-roofing projects. Epic Roofing & Construction LLC manages the entire permitting filing, fee coordination, and final code inspection schedule on your behalf.

What's the best time of year to replace a roof in Houston?

October through January offers the most stable installation window in Southeast Texas, featuring milder temperatures and lower storm frequency. Shingle thermal adhesive strips seal properly in mild weather without suffering from the blistering 100°F+ attic heat of midsummer or the volatile convective thunderstorms of spring.

How long does a roof last in Houston's climate?

Due to extreme UV radiation, 90%+ summer humidity, severe thermal expansion cycling, and periodic tropical storms, asphalt shingle roofs in Houston average a 15 to 20 year lifespan compared to the 20 to 25 year national average. Standing seam metal roofs routinely last 40 to 50+ years in the Houston coastal climate.
