Frequently Asked Galveston Roofing Questions

Are roofing installations in Galveston required to have a TWIA / TDI Windstorm Certificate (WPI-8)? ✕
Yes. Galveston Island is located within the Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) designated Catastrophe Area (Seaward Zone). Any roof replacement, structural repair, or re-decking must comply with strict coastal building codes and be inspected by an appointed Texas Licensed Professional Engineer or TDI windstorm inspector to receive a WPI-8 or WPI-8-E Certificate. This certificate is mandatory to maintain TWIA wind and hail insurance coverage.
What metal roofing materials best resist salt-air corrosion on Galveston Island? ✕
For coastal environments within 3,000 feet of open saltwater like the Gulf of Mexico or Galveston Bay, we recommend marine-grade aluminum standing seam panels (.032 or .040 gauge) or heavy-gauge Galvalume Plus coated with PVDF / Kynar 500 finishes. All fasteners must be 304 or 316 stainless steel or marine-grade coated clips to prevent galvanic corrosion and fastener shear.
What wind rating is needed for shingles installed in Galveston, TX? ✕
Galveston building codes require roofing systems engineered for 140 to 150+ MPH design wind speeds. We install GAF Timberline HDZ and UHDZ architectural shingles utilizing LayerLock technology and an enhanced 6-nail high-wind fastening schedule, combined with GAF DuraGrip sealant and full-coverage high-temp self-adhering ice-and-water underlayment.
How does Epic Roofing TX help Galveston property owners with hurricane and storm insurance claims? ✕
Our HAAG-certified forensic inspectors perform complete post-storm roof diagnostics, documenting wind uplift, shingle creasing, driven-rain deck infiltration, and salt-crust corrosion. We prepare itemized Xactimate estimates and meet your insurance adjuster or TWIA windstorm engineer directly on the roof to substantiate full replacement approval.
How quickly can your emergency response team reach Galveston Island after a tropical storm or hurricane? ✕
We maintain active coastal storm staging with emergency crews ready to dispatch across the I-45 causeway to Galveston Island, West End, Pirate's Beach, Jamaica Beach, and Pelican Island. We typically arrive within 2 to 4 hours post-storm to deploy commercial-grade water-tight emergency storm tarps.
